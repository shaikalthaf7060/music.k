## ✨ Features

- **🌟 3D Coverflow Carousel**: Interactive 3D spatial cards with smooth flipping and transitions.
- **🔍 Attractive Glass Search Bar**: Frosted glass capsule with instant search and clear button.
- **💊 Compact Floating Pill Player**: Snug, ergonomic bottom player bar with playback controls, soundwave bars, and artwork.
- **⏱️ Interactive Scrubber**: Click and drag anywhere along the timeline to jump to any part of the song with timestamp tooltips.
- **💫 Reactive Ambient Atmosphere**: Dynamic background glow that softly pulses on track changes.
- **🚫 100% Pure Audio Interface**: Zero video clutter or iframe frames.
- **🎤 Synchronized Karaoke Lyrics**: Real-time auto-scrolling lyrics.
- **🎛️ Equalizer & Visualizer**: 5-Band EQ and audio frequency visualizer.
